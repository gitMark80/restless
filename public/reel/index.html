How to record a reel
  1. Tap Load site. Wait for restless.faith to appear in the frame.
  2. Tap Ready. It says Fetching answer… for a few seconds while the real answer is fetched ahead of time, then Ready. The title card appears and everything else hides.
  3. Start your phone's screen recording from Control Center (long-press the record button and turn the microphone off so only the music is recorded), then swipe back.
  4. Tap the title card. The music starts, the question types itself in and the answer flows in right away, no waiting.
  5. While the answer comes in, the site is live: scroll up to show the whole answer and tap a source to open it. (Set End card after to Manual if you want unhurried time.)
  6. The end card appears when time is up, or tap the Restless.faith badge to end whenever you're done. Stop recording.
  7. Post the video as a Reel. If Control Center shows in the first second, trim it in the Reels editor.
